# Ella Blackwood — Character Sprite Prompts

> Amelia's best friend. 18, Black British (Afro-Caribbean heritage). The warmest visual presence in the game. She appears primarily through phone screens and in London chapters (1, 10, 12), so she needs fewer sprites but they must POP — she is colour and life against Plymouth's grey.

---

## MASTER REFERENCE — Character Sheet

```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A young woman, 18 years old, Black British, Afro-Caribbean heritage. Taller than average, athletic build. Beautiful natural curly afro hair, big and voluminous. Bright, expressive eyes — she always looks like she's about to laugh or fight. Warm dark skin. She wears colourful vintage finds — dungarees, Doc Martens, band tees. She is the most visually warm character in the story. Colour key: sunflower yellow and burnt orange. She radiates energy and fierce loyalty. Multiple expressions shown: joyful grin, fierce protective glare, worried but trying to hide it. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details
- **Hair:** Natural curly afro, big and voluminous. Sometimes in a headwrap.
- **Eyes:** Bright, expressive, always alive
- **Skin:** Warm dark tone
- **Build:** Taller than average, athletic
- **Signature:** Colourful vintage clothes, dungarees, Doc Martens
- **Palette:** Sunflower yellow #FFD700 / burnt orange — ALWAYS warm
- **Framing:** Waist-up, 3/4 view

## PROMPT TIPS

1. **Ella is WARMTH.** Even in her neutral state, she should feel like standing near a fire.
2. **Her palette must contrast Plymouth's grey.** When Ella appears in London chapters, the colour temperature shifts.
3. **She is not a stereotype.** She's a specific person — a Hackney girl who loves vintage clothes and has opinions. Not "sassy Black friend."
4. **Her hair is her visual signature** the way Amelia's jacket is hers. The afro must be consistent.
5. **She should look strong.** Not muscular-strong — confident-strong. She holds space.

# Supporting Characters — Sprite Prompts

> Characters with fewer scenes but distinct visual presence. Each needs a reference and 4 core expressions. Less variety needed but consistency still matters.

---

## TASHA

> The antagonist — but not a villain. Put-together, cold-pretty, weaponised social intelligence.

### Master Reference
```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style. A young woman, 19, white British. Blonde hair, styled and maintained — always looks like she spent time on it. Attractive in a polished, Instagram-ready way. Expensive casual clothes — the kind that look simple but cost too much. Colour key: cold pink and white. She looks like she could be your best friend or your worst enemy depending on what benefits her. There's something calculating behind the smile. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Expressions
**pleasant** (default) — Smiling, poised. The public face. It doesn't reach her eyes.
**cruel** — A smirk. Sharp eyes. She has found your weakness and she's enjoying it.
**exposed** — The mask slips. Brief flash of the insecurity underneath. Rare.
**angry** — Cold anger. Not hot — ice. She is furious but controlled.

## SOPHIA

> Academic, strategic, morally complex. She starts as Tasha's ally and becomes something more interesting.

### Master Reference
```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style. A young woman, 19, white British (or Southern European). Brunette, structured haircut. Academic chic — blazers, clean lines, everything intentional. She looks older than 19. Colour key: slate grey and emerald green. Intelligent eyes behind a composed exterior. She looks like she's playing a longer game than anyone else in the room. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Expressions
**composed** (default) — Neutral, watchful, put-together. Giving nothing away.
**calculating** — Eyes narrowed slightly, a micro-expression of strategy. She's deciding something.
**genuine** — Rare softness. The composure cracks and something real shows through. Used when she chooses honesty.
**conflicted** — Torn. Her eyes show one thing, her mouth another. She's not sure whose side she's on.

Short-cropped hair. He looks like he'd be intimidating if he weren't so clearly friendly. Colour key: red and navy. He wears sporty casual — team hoodies, joggers, trainers. ## CONSISTENCY RULES FOR ALL SUPPORTING CHARACTERS

1. **Same style prefix as main characters** — they must look like they belong in the same game.
2. **Fewer expressions = more important that each one is distinct.** If you only get 4, make sure they cover the emotional range the story needs.
3. **Tasha must look "normal."** She's not a cartoon villain. She's a real person who is cruel. That's scarier.
4. **The James family must look related.** Amelia shares features with Grace (Chinese heritage) and David's expressions.

# Lucas Holloway — Character Sprite Prompts

> Amelia's closest male friend at Plymouth. 18, white British (West Country). Tall, gentle, slightly awkward. The quiet intellectual who hides his vulnerability behind academic interest. He is warmth without pressure.

---

## MASTER REFERENCE — Character Sheet

```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A young man, 18 years old, white British. Tall, lanky build, slightly gangly — he hasn't quite grown into his height yet. Sandy floppy hair that needs cutting, falling across his forehead. He wears glasses that he occasionally pushes up or forgets. Kind face, gentle eyes. He wears academic-casual: a button-down shirt over a t-shirt, jeans, trainers. Colour key: navy and warm brown. He looks like someone who reads too much and worries about his friends. Comfortable, unthreatening, genuine. Multiple expressions shown: gentle smile, earnest concern, surprised, thinking. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details
- **Hair:** Sandy, floppy, needs cutting — falls across forehead
- **Eyes:** Kind, behind glasses
- **Build:** Tall, lanky, slightly awkward posture
- **Signature:** Button-down over t-shirt, glasses. Navy / warm brown palette.
- **Framing:** Waist-up, 3/4 view

# Sarah Whitmore — Character Sprite Prompts

> The most visually important supporting character. Sarah's appearance IS her arc — the player reads her deterioration through how she looks. Every sprite must be chapter-accurate. The wren tattoo on her inner left wrist must appear in every generation.

---

## MASTER REFERENCE — Character Sheet

```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A young woman, 18 years old, white British. Small, thin build. Mouse-brown straight hair, often tucked behind her ears. Grey-green eyes that are alert and observant in good moments. Pale skin. She wears oversized jumpers that hide her body, jeans, Converse trainers. Her visual palette is cool — grey and pale blue. She has a small hand-drawn wren tattoo on her inner left wrist — it is always visible. She is beautiful in a quiet, easily-missed way. She looks like someone who is paying attention to everything while hoping no one pays attention to her. Multiple states shown: present and gently smiling, withdrawn and looking down, flat and expressionless. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details (EVERY Sarah prompt)
- **Hair:** Mouse-brown, straight, tucked behind ears
- **Eyes:** Grey-green
- **Skin:** Pale, gets paler across chapters
- **Build:** Small, thin (gets thinner)
- **Signature:** Oversized jumper, Converse. The **wren tattoo** on inner left wrist.
- **Palette:** Always cooler than surrounding characters — grey, pale blue
- **Framing:** Waist-up, 3/4 view facing slightly right (mirrors Amelia's left-facing)

The shutting down. Eyes half-closed, looking down. De-animating.

### present ⚠️ KEY EXPRESSION
The opposite of withdrawn. Alert, engaged, HERE. Rare after Ch5.
```

### flat ⚠️ MOST IMPORTANT EXPRESSION
No expression at all. The scariest one. Used in late chapters.
```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style. Young woman, 18, white British, small and thin, mouse-brown straight hair — lank, unwashed, falling flat. Grey-green eyes that are open but not seeing. Wearing the same oversized jumper. Small wren tattoo visible on inner left wrist. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view. Expression: flat — completely blank. No emotion readable. Eyes open, mouth closed, nothing. She is not present. This should be unsettling. The most desaturated, coolest version of her palette. Dark circles under her eyes. Soft even lighting, flat neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic.

## VISUAL ARC — The Deterioration

This is the most important visual storytelling in the entire game. Sarah's appearance tracks her mental health deterioration. Each chapter group needs its own sprite set.

| Chapters | Hair | Body | Face | Jumper | The Tell |
|----------|------|------|------|--------|----------|
| 2–3 (arrival) | Clean, neat, tucked behind ears | Thin but healthy | Alert, present, occasionally smiling | Clean, fitting | She makes eye contact |
| 4–5 (settling) | Slightly less neat | Thinner | Less eye contact, slower to smile | Same jumper appearing more often | She starts missing meals |
| 6 (withdrawal) | Unwashed, lank, falling flat | Visibly thinner | Dark circles, pale, vacant | Same jumper, worn repeatedly, sleeve-pulling | Fairy lights are off in her room |
| 7 (approach) | Same as 6, worse | Bones visible at wrist near tattoo | The light has gone from her eyes | Same jumper | She has stopped trying to look okay |
| 8 (crisis) | Depends on outcome | Depends on outcome | Depends on outcome | — | — |
| 9+ (if alive) | Slowly recovering — clean again | Slowly gaining weight | A glimmer returning | Different jumper (a new start) | She says yes to things |

### Chapter 6 Sprite — The Turning Point
This is when the player should NOTICE. Generate Sarah's Ch6 sprite to be clearly different from Ch2-3.
```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style. Young woman, 18, white British, notably thin, mouse-brown hair hanging lank and unwashed. Grey-green eyes with dark circles beneath them, vacant gaze. Wearing the same oversized grey jumper — it looks larger on her now because she has lost weight. Sleeves pulled over her hands. Small wren tattoo barely visible at the wrist. She is pale, almost grey. The life has receded from her face. She looks like someone who has stopped taking care of herself. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view. Heavily desaturated cool palette. Soft even lighting, flat neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

## OUTFIT VARIANTS

- **Outfit A:** Oversized cream jumper, jeans, Converse. Hair clips sometimes. Looks like she tried.
- **Outfit B:** A cardigan over a t-shirt. Slightly more dressed up — she's making an effort.

### Late (Ch6–8)
- **Outfit C:** The same jumper. Just the jumper. Sleeves stretched over hands. Jeans. The Converse are dirty. She has stopped changing.

### Recovery (Ch9+, if alive)
- **Outfit D:** A different jumper — something new. Clean. Hair washed. Still oversized, still Sarah, but it's a fresh start.

## THE WREN TATTOO

A small, hand-drawn style wren on the inner left wrist. Simple linework — not a professional tattoo parlour job. More like something a friend drew. It is a **key visual motif** that recurs throughout the game (see art_direction.md §11). It must be visible in every Sarah sprite where her wrist is in frame.

```
Reference prompt for the tattoo: A small hand-drawn wren bird tattoo on the inner wrist. Simple black linework, slightly uneven — like a friend's drawing, not a parlour piece. The wren is tiny, detailed enough to recognise as a bird but small enough to miss if you're not looking. In Celtic folklore, the wren is the king of birds despite being the smallest.
```

## PROMPT TIPS

1. **Sarah must NOT look "pretty-sad."** AI loves to make sadness glamorous. She should look *unwell*, not *aesthetic*.
2. **The weight loss must be visible.** Compare Ch2 to Ch6 — the jumper should look larger because she is smaller.
3. **Generate her arc as a sequence.** Ch2 → Ch4 → Ch6 → Ch8 (surviving) → Ch9. See the change.
4. **She should always be the coolest palette in any scene.** Even in warm environments, Sarah runs cold.
5. **The flat expression is the hardest to get right.** It's not sleeping, not sad, not angry — it's *nothing*. Keep generating until it's genuinely unsettling.
6. **She mirrors Amelia physically.** Both are small, dark-haired, quiet. But Sarah is paler, thinner, cooler. They are foils.

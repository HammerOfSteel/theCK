# Raj Patel — Character Sprite Prompts

> 18, British-Indian. The heart of the flat. Stocky, solid, always near the kitchen. He is comfort and loyalty made visible. He smells like his own cooking and no one has ever had a bad word to say about him.

---

## MASTER REFERENCE

```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A young man, 18 years old, British-Indian. Stocky, solid build — not fat, just built like someone who eats well and doesn't overthink it. Black hair, short and neat. Open, warm face. He wears comfortable clothes — joggers, hoodies, trainers. Sometimes an apron over it. Colour key: warm terracotta and cream. He looks like the person you'd want in a crisis because he'd make you a cup of tea and tell you it would be alright, and you'd believe him. Multiple expressions: easy grin, concerned but steady, focused (cooking), gentle. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details
- **Hair:** Black, short, neat
- **Eyes:** Warm, open
- **Build:** Stocky, solid
- **Signature:** Comfortable clothes, apron variant, warm palette
- **Framing:** Waist-up, 3/4 view

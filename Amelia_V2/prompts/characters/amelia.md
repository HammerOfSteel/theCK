# Amelia James — Character Sprite Prompts

> Protagonist. 18, mixed heritage (Chinese-British). The player sees through her eyes but also sees her in CGs and sprites. She must look like a real person navigating a real year — not a heroine, not a waif. A young woman finding out who she is.

---

## MASTER REFERENCE — Character Sheet

Use this prompt first to establish Amelia's look. Generate 3-4 variations and pick the one that feels right. This becomes the **anchor image** — reference it (or describe it) in every subsequent prompt to maintain consistency.

```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A young woman, 18 years old, mixed Chinese-British heritage. Average height, slim build. Black hair, shoulder-length, loosely tied back with a few strands escaping. Dark brown expressive eyes. Warm skin tone #D4A574. She has an intelligent, slightly guarded face — the kind of person who watches before she speaks. She wears a well-worn denim jacket over a simple jumper, dark jeans, ankle boots. A canvas tote bag over one shoulder. No makeup or minimal. She looks like a first-year university student in England, not a model. Multiple expressions shown: neutral, smiling gently, anxious, determined. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. Slightly impressionistic brushwork. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details (include in EVERY Amelia prompt)
These details must remain constant across all generations:
- **Hair:** Black, shoulder-length, loosely tied back
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, expressive
- **Skin:** Warm tone, mixed Chinese-British heritage
- **Build:** Average height, slim
- **Signature items:** Denim jacket, canvas tote bag
- **Framing:** Waist-up, 3/4 view facing slightly left (consistent across all expressions)

---

## EXPRESSIONS

Each expression prompt is the anchor + emotion modifier. Generate these as a batch for consistency.

### Sprite Format
All sprites: **600×900px portrait, transparent or flat neutral background** (for compositing over scene backgrounds in Ren'Py). Waist-up, 3/4 view facing slightly left.

## OUTFIT VARIANTS

### Casual (Chapters 1–7, default)
The denim jacket look. This is Amelia's visual identity.
- **Outfit A (autumn/winter):** Denim jacket, warm jumper underneath (cream or olive), dark jeans, ankle boots. Canvas tote bag.
- **Outfit B (going out):** Same jacket but nicer top underneath — a button-down blouse or similar. Hair slightly neater.
- **Outfit C (home/tired):** Oversized hoodie, joggers. Hair fully down. No bag. This is flat-Amelia, late-night-essay-Amelia.

### Academic (lectures/library)
- **Outfit D:** The jacket but with a scarf, maybe a notebook visible in hand. Slightly more put-together.

### Chapter 8 - Crisis
- **Outfit E (the ordeal):** Rumpled. Whatever she was wearing before the call — untucked, hair messy, dark circles under eyes. She has not slept.

### Chapter 12 - Endings
- **Outfit F (summer):** Lighter. No jacket (it's June). A simple top, lighter jeans or a skirt. Hair might be slightly longer. She looks *different* — not dramatically, but the player should notice she's grown.

## PROMPT TIPS FOR CONSISTENCY

1. **Always reference your anchor image.** If your generator supports image-to-image or reference, use the character sheet as input.
2. **Keep the 3/4 view consistent.** Don't switch to front-facing or profile between expressions.
3. **Generate expressions in batches.** Doing all 12 in one session reduces drift.
4. **The denim jacket is the anchor.** If the jacket is recognisable, the character is recognisable.
5. **Check: does she look 18?** AI tends to age up or age down. She should look like a first-year uni student — not 15, not 25.
6. **Check: does she look mixed heritage?** Not "half-Asian stereotype." A real person who has a Chinese mum and a white British dad. The features should be natural, not exoticised.

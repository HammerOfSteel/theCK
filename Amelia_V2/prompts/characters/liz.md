# Liz — Character Sprite Prompts

> The first friend at Plymouth. Bright, energetic, surf-adjacent. She's the welcome committee — warm and easy-going. Fewer sprite needs (supporting role from Ch4 onwards).

---

## MASTER REFERENCE

```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A young woman, around 18-19, white British. Bright, energetic presence. Hair in a messy bun — sun-lightened brown. Tanned, outdoorsy look — she surfs or at least lives near the sea. She wears casual, slightly beach-adjacent clothes — a fleece over a vest, cropped trousers, flip-flops or trainers. Colour key: turquoise and sandy yellow. She looks like a Devon girl who grew up running barefoot. Easy smile, uncomplicated warmth. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

## EXPRESSIONS

### neutral
```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style. Young woman, ~18, white British, messy bun, tanned, wearing a fleece over a vest top. Waist-up, 3/4 view. Expression: neutral — easy-going, open, slightly smiling. She is chill. Turquoise and sandy yellow palette. Flat neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### happy


### worried
```
Same anchor. Expression: worried — brow furrowed, bite of her lower lip. She's concerned but doesn't know what to do about it.
```

### surprised
```
Same anchor. Expression: surprised — eyes wide, mouth in an "O." She didn't expect that.
```

### laughing
```
Same anchor. Expression: laughing — head tilted, eyes crinkled, bright and easy. Liz laughs easily and often.
```

### concerned
```
Same anchor. Expression: concerned — eyes soft, mouth set. She can see something's wrong with a friend. She doesn't have the words for it, but she cares.
```

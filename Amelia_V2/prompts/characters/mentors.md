# Mentors — Character Sprite Prompts

> Four mentor paths, four very different visual presences. Each mentor embodies a different way of knowing, and their appearance should telegraph that before they speak a word.

---

## PROFESSOR HAWTHORNE

### Master Reference
```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A man in his late 50s, white British. Tall, thin, slightly stooped — carries himself like someone who has been reading for forty years. Grey thinning hair swept back from a lined, intelligent forehead. Reading glasses pushed up on his forehead (never on his nose in the default). Wears a worn tweed jacket with elbow patches, open-collar shirt, no tie. Academic shabby-genteel — everything is quality but nothing is new. Colour key: muted olive and warm grey. He looks like he's heard everything and is still interested. Wry mouth. Sharp eyes. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details
- **Hair:** Grey, thinning, swept back
- **Eyes:** Sharp, observant — glasses on forehead
- **Build:** Tall, thin, stooped
- **Signature:** Worn tweed jacket, glasses on forehead
- **Palette:** Muted olive / warm grey

### Expressions
**wry_amusement** (default) — Slight smile, eyebrow cocked. He finds everything faintly amusing.
**sharp_focus** — Glasses pulled down to nose, eyes narrowed. He's caught something in your argument.
**rare_warmth** — The wry mask drops. Genuine, undefended kindness. Used maybe three times in the game.
**devastating_honesty** — Level gaze, no smile. He is about to say something that will change how you think.
**disappointed** — Not angry. Worse. He expected better and you both know it.
**teaching** — Animated, gesturing with one hand, eyes bright. He loves this.

## DR. SIMMONS

### Master Reference
```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A woman in her early 40s, British South Asian heritage. Medium height, rounded build — warm and approachable in body as in manner. Dark hair in a neat braid or low bun. She wears professional but soft clothes — cardigans in warm colours, nice scarves, sensible shoes. The world's best listener. Colour key: warm burgundy and cream. She looks like someone who has sat with a lot of pain and knows how to hold space for it without being consumed. Kind eyes, patient mouth. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details
- **Hair:** Dark, neat braid or low bun
- **Eyes:** Kind, patient
- **Build:** Medium, rounded, warm
- **Signature:** Cardigan, scarf, professional warmth
- **Palette:** Burgundy / cream

### Expressions
**patient_listening** (default) — Calm, open, fully attentive. This is active listening made visible.
**gentle_challenge** — One eyebrow slightly raised, slight lean forward. She's about to ask the question you've been avoiding.
**quiet_delight** — Eyes brightening, a soft smile. You've had a breakthrough and she sees it.
**rare_tears** — Eyes glistening. Used once. When something Amelia says hits her professionally or personally.
**serious** — The warmth is still there but there's gravity. Something needs to be said carefully.
**encouraging** — Full warm smile. She believes in you.

## MAYA PENROSE

### Master Reference
```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A woman in her mid 30s, white British (Cornish). Small, wiry, kinetic — she moves like she's always about to go somewhere. Long dark red hair, often loose, occasionally braided with ribbons or thread. She wears bohemian-practical clothes — long skirts, boots, layers, lots of rings. A stone necklace. She is NOT a hippie caricature — she's a working academic who knows where the ley lines run. Sharp intelligence in her eyes, grounded energy. Colour key: deep green and earthen red. She looks like she could teach a university seminar and then walk barefoot on Bodmin Moor without changing gear. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details
- **Hair:** Long, dark red, often loose. Ribbons/thread braids sometimes.
- **Eyes:** Sharp, intelligent, alive
- **Build:** Small, wiry, energetic
- **Signature:** Bohemian layers, rings, stone necklace
- **Palette:** Deep green / earthen red
- **WARNING:** She must NOT look like a crystal-shop cliché.

### Expressions
**warm_welcome** (default) — Open face, ready smile. She draws people in naturally.
**intense_focus** — Eyes fixed, still. The energy has concentrated. Something matters here.
**mysterious** — A knowing look. She knows something she's not saying and she's fine with that.
**grounded** — Calm, centred, feet-on-the-earth steady. The anchor in a storm.
**teaching** — Animated, hands moving with objects or gestures. Passionate. She loves what she studies.
**concerned** — The groundedness wavers. She's worried about a student.

## ELENA VOSKRESENSKAYA

### Master Reference
```
CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET — Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, contemporary British literary illustration. A woman in her 70s, Slavic features (Russian émigré heritage, settled in Cornwall decades ago). Small, sharp, wiry — she looks like she's made of wire and willpower. White hair, cropped short, no-nonsense. Practical Cornish country clothes — a waxed jacket outside, wellies. Inside her cottage: a thick shawl, always cold. Her face is deeply lined and beautiful in the way only a life fully lived makes a face beautiful. Colour key: storm grey and amber candle glow. She looks like she could stare through you and see what you're made of. Waist-up portrait, 3/4 view, soft neutral background. No anime, no photorealistic, no cartoon.
```

### Anchor Details
- **Hair:** White, cropped short
- **Eyes:** Dark, sharp, seeing
- **Build:** Small, wiry, tough
- **Signature:** Shawl (indoors), waxed jacket (outdoors). Deeply lined face.
- **Palette:** Storm grey / amber candlelight
- **NOTE:** Elena has TWO visual modes — outdoors (cold grey) and indoors (warm amber). Same face, radically different palette.

### Expressions
**appraising** (default) — One eye slightly narrowed, mouth neutral. She is measuring you.
**amused** — A dry smile, barely a twitch. She knows something you'll learn in twenty years.
**stern** — Direct, unblinking. No warmth. You have made an error and she will not pretend otherwise.
**vulnerable** — Used ONCE, maybe twice. The armour cracks. Her eyes go soft and distant. She is remembering something. This is the most powerful image of Elena.
**teaching** — Intensity. Leaning forward, hands perhaps on a book or stone. She is passing something on and it matters enormously.
**gentle** — The rarest. A genuine softness that transforms her face. She cares deeply; she just doesn't advertise it.

## PROMPT TIPS FOR ALL MENTORS

1. **Age them correctly.** AI tends to make older characters look 35 or look 90. Hawthorne is 55-60, Simmons is 40-42, Maya is 33-36, Elena is 72-78.
2. **They must look distinct from each other immediately.** Put all four reference images side by side — if you can't tell them apart at thumbnail size, redo.
3. **Each mentor's palette should match their knowledge domain:** Hawthorne (olive/grey = academia), Simmons (burgundy/cream = therapy), Maya (green/red = earth/folklore), Elena (grey/amber = occult/ancient).
4. **Elena must NOT look witchy in the Hollywood sense.** No crystal ball, no black cats (she has a grey cat, ordinary), no pointy anything. She looks like a Cornish grandmother because she almost is one.

# Plymouth Campus — Background Prompts

> The University of Plymouth — brutalist 1960s architecture softened by coastal light. These backgrounds carry the Albedo palette (Chapters 4–7) predominantly, with Nigredo bleeding in early (Ch2–3) and Citrinitas/Rubedo arriving late (Ch8–12).

---

## STYLE PREFIX (all campus backgrounds)

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape, contemporary British literary illustration, atmospheric lighting. University of Plymouth, Devon, England. Brutalist architecture, coastal light. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

---

## bg_plymouth_first_sight
**Used:** Ch2 (first arrival)
**Phase:** Late Nigredo / early Albedo — transition

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. The approach to Plymouth from the A38 — first sight of the city. The urban sprawl visible ahead, the Tamar Bridge or Plym estuary in the distance. Grey September sky, a hint of sea light. The city is neither beautiful nor ugly — it's real, concrete, coastal. This is where Amelia's new life begins and it looks like a city, not a dream. Colour palette: road grey, sky grey-blue, city concrete softened by sea light. Contemporary British literary illustration. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

## MENTOR OFFICES

### bg_hawthorne_office
**Used:** Ch4, 7, 8, slideshows
**Phase:** Albedo — but warm from books

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. A professor's office at the University of Plymouth. Not large — a desk overflowing with papers, books on every wall floor to ceiling, a reading lamp casting warm light. A window showing grey campus outside. An old leather chair. Stacks of student essays. A mug with cold tea. The office of someone who has been thinking in this room for twenty years. Colour palette: warm olive-grey, book-spine brown, desk-lamp amber against institutional grey. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. A university counsellor's office — purposefully welcoming.

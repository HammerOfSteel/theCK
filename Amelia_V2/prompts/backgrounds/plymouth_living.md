# Plymouth Living Spaces — Background Prompts

> Student accommodation — halls (Ch2–6), shared house (Ch7+), and individual rooms. These are the most intimate spaces in the game. The kitchen is where relationships happen.

---

## bg_halls_kitchen_evening
**Used:** Ch2 (the flatmates meet — one of the most important backgrounds in the game)
**Phase:** Late Nigredo / early Albedo

The room where everything begins. Every flat scene runs through this kitchen.

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. Interior of a student halls kitchen in Plymouth — shared between 6-8 students. Small, crowded, functional. Cheap countertops, a microwave, mismatched mugs. A notice board on the wall with house rules and takeaway menus. Too many people for the space — that's the point. Warm overhead light (slightly yellow tungsten). Through the window: evening darkness, campus lights. This room is ugly and vital. Everything important happens here. Colour palette: magnolia walls, institutional blue carpet implied, warm tungsten light, the colour of whoever is cooking (Raj's spices, warm). Contemporary British literary illustration. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

# London Backgrounds — Image Prompts

> London appears in Chapters 1 (departure), 6 (Christmas return, brief), 10 (homecoming), and 12 (endings). The palette shifts dramatically: Ch1 is Nigredo (warm darks, gold lamplight), Ch10/12 is Rubedo (wine, rose, golden hour). Same places, different Amelia.

**Used:** Ch1 (the family dinner before Amelia leaves)
**Phase:** Nigredo — warm darks, amber lamplight

The emotional heart of the James household. Small, warm, lived-in.

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. Interior of a small kitchen in a Hackney terraced house, evening. Warm tungsten lighting from an overhead bulb. A rice cooker on the counter, a Chinese calendar on the wall, a small table with mismatched chairs. Clean but well-used — signs of a family that eats together every night. Through the small window, the amber glow of London streetlights. Colour palette: burnt umber, dark gold, charcoal brown. The room feels full even when empty. Warm, domestic, soon to be missed. Contemporary British literary illustration. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

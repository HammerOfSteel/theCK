# Cornwall — Background Prompts

> Cornwall is a character. The landscape art is the most detailed element of the game — the sea, the moor, the stone, the light. These places are ancient, real, and specific. Use actual location names in prompts for AI grounding. Always include "Cornwall, England" to prevent American visual defaults.

---

## STYLE PREFIX (all Cornwall backgrounds)

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape, contemporary British literary illustration. Cornwall, England. Ancient landscape, Atlantic light. The most atmospheric backgrounds in the game. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

## bg_cornwall_coast
**Used:** Ch5 (group trip), Ch9, 11, 12, slideshows — the most-used Cornwall background
**Phase:** Varies across chapters

The Atlantic coast of Cornwall. Cliffs, sea, sky, wind.

```
ALBEDO VERSION (Ch5 — first visit, autumn):
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. The Atlantic coast of Cornwall, England. Dramatic granite cliffs dropping to a grey-green sea. White surf breaking against rocks below. A coastal path winding along the clifftop, rough grass and gorse. October light — pale, vast, the sky bigger than anywhere Amelia has been. Wind visible in the grass. The scale is humbling — this landscape does not care about your problems. Colour palette: Albedo — sea grey, granite silver, pale sky, gorse gold accent. Contemporary British literary illustration. No anime, no photorealistic.

RUBEDO VERSION (Ch11-12 — late spring/summer):
Same composition but transformed by season and light. The sea is deep blue and turquoise. The gorse is in full bloom (yellow-gold). The cliffs are warm in afternoon sun. The same path, the same view, but the world has warmed. Colour palette: Rubedo — sea green #2E8B57, gold bloom, warm stone, wide blue sky. This is Cornwall showing itself to someone who has earned it.
```

## bg_men_an_tol
**Used:** Ch4 (first visit with mentor), Ch12 (return)
**Phase:** Albedo (Ch4), Rubedo (Ch12)

Three standing stones on open moorland. The holed stone (the Men) is central — a granite ring you can pass through.

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. Mên-an-Tol, Cornwall, England. Three ancient granite standing stones on open moorland. The central stone is a circle — a ring of stone with a hole large enough to pass through, standing on edge. Two upright stones flank it. The stone is grey-green with lichen. Rough grass and heather around the base. The sky is wide and the land is flat. These stones have been here since the Neolithic — they are older than English. They should feel matter-of-fact, not mystical. They just are. Colour palette: granite grey-green, moor grass, lichen gold, wide sky.

Ch4 VERSION: Albedo — cool daylight, autumn. The stones are strange and real.
Ch12 VERSION: Rubedo — golden hour. The stones are the same. Amelia is not.

No anime, no photorealistic.
```

**Used:** Ch4, slideshows

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. The Merry Maidens stone circle, Cornwall, England. **Used:** Ch4 (Elena/Maya path), Ch7, Ch8, slideshows
**Phase:** Albedo — but green-gold (the well has its own light)

A holy well deep in ancient woodland. Clootie rags on the branches.

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. Madron Holy Well, Cornwall, England. Deep in a wood — the path is muddy, the trees ancient and close. A ruined medieval baptistry made of stone, half-swallowed by vegetation. The well is a dark pool of clear water set in mossy stone. Clootie rags — strips of cloth — tied to the branches overhead, faded, weathering, some rotting. They are prayers made visible. Dappled light filtering through leaves — green-gold. The place is eerie and beautiful and very old. It smells of earth and water. Colour palette: green-gold dapple, moss green, dark water, faded cloth colours, stone grey. The feeling of stepping sideways out of time. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. The entrance to Carn Euny Fogou, Cornwall, England. ## ELENA'S COTTAGE — Special Location

The most important single set for the Elena path. Split into exterior and interior.

### Exterior
```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. A small granite cottage on the clifftop near Zennor, Cornwall, England. Stone walls, slate roof, wind-bent trees around it. A garden of herbs and wildflowers — not decorative, functional. A path leading to the cliff edge and the sea beyond. Smoke from the chimney. The cottage is old and solid and belongs to the landscape — it grew here. The sky is wide and the wind is always. Colour palette: granite grey, slate, garden green, wildflower colour, wide Atlantic sky. This is where someone who knows things lives. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Interior
```
Painterly illustration, soft oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape. Interior of Elena's Cornish stone cottage — one central room with a stone fireplace, fire lit, casting warm orange light. Thick stone walls. Low ceiling with dried herbs and plants hanging from wooden beams. Bookshelves crammed with old volumes. A worn wooden table with a pestle and mortar, scattered papers, a magnifying glass. Jars of herbs and tinctures on shelves. A grey cat curled in an old armchair. One candle on the table. The room feels like decades of quiet knowledge — warm, crowded, lived-in, not staged. NO crystals, NO pentagrams, NO mystical props — this is a working scholar's home that happens to study things most scholars don't. Colour palette: amber firelight, grey stone walls, dark wood, book-spine colour, dried-herb greens and browns. Like walking into a different century. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

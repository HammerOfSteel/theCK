# CG Event Art — Image Prompts

> Full-screen 1920×1080 illustrations for the game's emotional peaks. These are the images players screenshot and remember. Each must be more detailed and painterly than the sprites or backgrounds. Leave textbox-safe space in the bottom 15%.

---

## STYLE PREFIX (all CGs)

```
Painterly illustration, rich oil painting style, 1920x1080 landscape, contemporary British literary illustration. Full illustration — more detailed and atmospheric than sprite art. Faces clearly expressive. Dramatic lighting. Leave the bottom 15% of the composition slightly darker/simpler for textbox overlay. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

## CG 1 — Thames at Night (Ch1)
**The game's opening image. Melancholy, departure, gold on black water.**

```
Painterly illustration, rich oil painting style, 1920x1080. A young woman (Amelia — 18, mixed Chinese-British heritage, black hair, denim jacket) standing at the South Bank railing on the Thames at night, London. She faces the water, back mostly to the camera, slight 3/4 profile visible. City lights of the North Bank reflected on the black river — shattered gold on obsidian. The railing is iron, cold under her hands. Her best friend (Ella — 18, Black British, voluminous natural curly afro, colourful jacket) stands beside her, looking at Amelia rather than the water. The mood is honest and melancholy — this is goodbye. The water is the nigredo. Colour palette: Nigredo — black water, city gold reflections, midnight blue sky, Ella's warm colour against the cold. Contemporary British literary illustration. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

## CG 3 — The Bench (Ch3)
**The most important recurring image. Amelia and Sarah, the sea, the truth.**

```
Painterly illustration, rich oil painting style, 1920x1080. Wide shot — WIDE. Plymouth Hoe, October afternoon. A weathered wooden bench faces the vast grey English Channel. Two young women sit on the bench — small figures against the enormous sea and sky. Amelia (denim jacket, dark hair) on the left, Sarah (oversized jumper, mouse-brown hair, smaller) on the right. They are not looking at each other — both face the sea. The space between them is honest. Smeaton's Tower visible in the distance. Grey cloud, cold light, wind. The composition should make them SMALL — the sea is bigger than anything. The mood is quiet, cold, and the most honest moment so far. Colour palette: Albedo — sea grey, chalk sky, cool blue, the bench warm-brown as the only warm object. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

## CG 11 — Fogou Interior (Ch11, Elena path)
**The most important single image for the occult path. Transcendence.**

```
Painterly illustration, rich oil painting style, 1920x1080. Inside Carn Euny Fogou, Cornwall. A stone-lined underground passage — ancient walls of massive granite blocks. Torchlight (or candlelight) casting amber on the stone. Carvings barely visible in the stone — cup marks, spirals, worn by millennia. The passage feels close, cold, womb-like. Not a dungeon, not a cave — the body of the land itself. Generate FOUR tiers:

TIER 1 (low OK stat): Unsettling — the shadows are too deep, the stones press in. The air is wrong.
TIER 2 (medium): Clarifying — the candlelight is steady, the stones are solid. Something is being understood.
TIER 3 (high OK): Numinous — the candlelight multiplies, the passage seems to open. The carvings glow faintly with reflected light. Something old is paying attention.
TIER 4 (highest OK): Transcendent — golden light fills the passage from no visible source. The stones are alive. Amelia's face is illuminated with understanding.

No anime, no photorealistic.
```

## CG 12 — Endings (Ch12)
**Seven variants. The most important images in the game.**

Each ending needs its own composition:

### Grief Ending
```
Amelia alone on the bench at Plymouth Hoe. The same bench from CG 3. But it's one person now. Winter. The sea is grey and the bench is empty beside her. Everything is Nigredo — the colour never warmed. She is looking at the sea. She survived, but she is carrying something. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Alchemist Ending
```
Amelia at Mên-an-Tol, golden hour. She stands beside the holed stone, one hand on the ancient granite. The light is gold and strange. She is looking through the hole — what she sees on the other side is up to interpretation. Cornwall in full Rubedo glory. She found something most people never find. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Scholar Ending
```
Amelia in a library or study — but not the university one. A better one. Books around her, a laptop, notes, a cup of tea. Afternoon sun through a window. She is working, absorbed, content. The academic life, chosen and loved. Piano light — clear, resolving. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Companion Ending
```
Amelia surrounded by her people — Lucas, Zara, Raj, maybe Ella visiting. The flat kitchen or a pub table. Laughter. Full warmth. These are her family now. Group portrait, candid, everyone mid-gesture. The warmest image in the game. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Healer Ending
```
Amelia and Sarah on the bench — BUT Sarah is alive and present. They sit together, closer than in CG 3. Sarah is looking at the sea with a real expression — not flat, not vacant. Present. Spring light. The bench is the same but the world is warmer. Gentle, strings-and-light. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Whole Ending
```
Amelia walking along the Cornwall coastal path with the sea and sky wide around her. She is walking toward something — camera behind her, her face in profile, lit by golden hour. Everything she's learned is in her posture — open, grounded, forward. All the colours from every phase present in the sky and landscape. The whole palette, integrated. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

### Bittersweet Ending
```
Amelia at the train station, heading back to Plymouth. She stands on the platform, looking down the tracks toward the future. Her bag on her shoulder. It's raining lightly but the sky is breaking open in the distance — sun through cloud. She's smiling slightly. It's not a happy ending or a sad ending. It's a real one. The main theme in a minor key, then fading. No anime, no photorealistic.
```

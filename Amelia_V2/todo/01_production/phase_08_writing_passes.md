# Phase 08 — Writing & Narrative Passes

> *Goal: Bring every chapter's prose, pacing, and voice to final quality **before** voice generation and
> art creation lock text in place.*
> This is the polish work formerly listed under Phase 3.5.4.

---

## 8.1 Line-Level Craft
- [ ] **Dialogue polish** — Read all dialogue aloud. Cut anything that reads like a lecture.
- [ ] **Character voice audit** — Every character identifiable by dialogue alone (cross-check
      `design/dialogue_style_guide.md`)
- [ ] **British register / age check** — 18–22-year-old university voices; slang current, not cringe
- [ ] **Proofread pass** — Spelling, grammar, punctuation, consistent en/em-dash + quote style

## 8.2 Structure & Pacing
- [ ] **Pacing review** — Each chapter: rising/falling tension, no two adjacent scenes at the same pitch
- [ ] **Scene-length balance** — Flag walls of text; break up or add choice beats
- [ ] **Thematic consistency** — Alchemical / Jungian threads woven subtly, never stated outright
      (cross-check `design/alchemical_thread_map.md`)
- [ ] **Continuity sweep** — Season, weather, wardrobe, time-of-day, and prior-choice callbacks are consistent

## 8.3 Branching & Choice Integrity
- [ ] **Choice-map reconciliation** — Every choice in scripts matches `design/choice_map.md` point values
- [ ] **Relationship-gate check** — Conditional branches fire at intended thresholds (`design/relationship_matrix.md`)
- [ ] **Dead-end / orphan check** — No choice leads to a missing label or unintended fallthrough
- [ ] **Point-balance validation** — Simulate the 6 playstyles vs. `design/point_balance_spreadsheet.md`;
      confirm every ending is reachable and no ending is trivially forced

## 8.4 Sensitivity & Care
- [ ] **Content sensitivity review** — Sarah's arc, racism, bullying, depression, grief — accurate and careful
      (cross-check `design/sarahs_arc.md` tone guidelines + content warnings)
- [ ] **Content-warning coverage** — Every flagged scene is gated by the content-warning screen
- [ ] **Support-resources screen** — Real-world signposting (helplines) accessible from menu, region-appropriate

---

**Dependency note:** This phase should be **substantially locked per chapter before** that chapter's voice
lines (Phase 07) are generated, to avoid re-recording. Art (Phase 14) can proceed in parallel once text is stable.

**Exit criteria:** Text is final and locked; choice map, relationship gates, and point balance all validate;
sensitivity review signed off.

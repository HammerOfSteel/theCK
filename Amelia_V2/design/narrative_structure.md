# The CK: Amelia V2 — Narrative Structure

> This document maps every chapter, act, scene, and choice point. It is the blueprint from which the actual writing will be done. Each chapter follows the Hero's Journey stage it represents while maintaining a naturalistic, character-driven story.

---

## CHAPTER 1: THE ORDINARY WORLD
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero in their familiar world before the adventure begins.
**Alchemical Stage:** Pre-Nigredo — the unexamined life, the *prima materia* in its raw state.
**Month:** Late September. **Location:** London.
**Mood:** Golden, nostalgic, bittersweet. The last days of summer. Everything is familiar but already feels like it's slipping away.

### Act 1: The Golden Afternoon
**Scene 1.1 — The Park Bench**
Amelia sits in her local park in Bromley. Late September light. She's reading a psychology textbook — she's already started before the course begins. Internal monologue establishes her: curious, slightly anxious, eager.

**Choice 1:** What is Amelia reading?
- A) *Thinking, Fast and Slow* by Kahneman → AA +1 ("I want to understand how minds work")
- B) *Man and His Symbols* by Jung → SD +1 ("I want to understand what dreams mean")
- C) *The Tibetan Book of Living and Dying* → OK +1 ("I picked this up and couldn't put it down")

**Scene 1.2 — Ella's Call**
Phone buzzes. It's Ella. They chat about Amelia leaving — Ella is staying in London for uni. The dialogue is warm, funny, real. Ella is worried about losing her best friend but won't say it directly.

**Choice 2:** How does Amelia respond to Ella's unspoken worry?
- A) "We'll FaceTime every day, I promise." → SI +1 (comforting but possibly unrealistic)
- B) "It's going to be weird, isn't it? Not seeing each other all the time." → MH +1 (honest acknowledgment)

### Act 2: The Family
**Scene 1.3 — Dinner with the Family**
The James household. Mum (Grace) has cooked Amelia's favourite meal — ackee and saltfish. Dad (David) is quiet but keeps glancing at her. Lily is there too, texting under the table.

**Choice 3:** The conversation turns to what Amelia expects from university.
- A) "I want to be the first person in our family with a degree." → AA +1, MC +1
- B) "I just want to figure out who I am, honestly." → SD +1, MH +1
- C) "I've been reading about some really interesting stuff — psychology, philosophy, even some older traditions..." → OK +1, SD +1

**Scene 1.4 — Packing the Room**
Amelia packs her childhood bedroom. Each object is a memory. This scene establishes her world through what she chooses to take.

**Choice 4:** One last item to pack — she can only fit one more thing:
- A) Her psychology flashcards (she's been making them all summer) → AA +1
- B) A photo of her and Ella at their Year 11 prom → SI +1
- C) A journal — blank, for whatever comes next → SD +1

### Act 3: The Threshold's Edge
**Scene 1.5 — The Bookshop**
Amelia visits the Bromley bookshop one last time. The owner, Mr. Patel (no relation to Maya — or is there?), recommends a farewell gift.

**Choice 5:** Which book catches her eye?
- A) A modern psychology anthology → AA +1
- B) *Letters to a Young Poet* by Rilke → MH +1 ("You must change your life")
- C) An old, leather-bound volume in the "Esoterica" section — *The Aurora of the Philosophers* by Paracelsus. A note is tucked inside the cover. → OK +1
  - **(CRITICAL: This choice is one of three requirements for unlocking Elena as mentor)**

**Scene 1.6 — The Thames at Night**
Amelia walks along the river alone. London is lit up. The water is dark and reflective. Internal monologue: she is standing on the edge of her old life.

**Choice 6:** What does she think about?
- A) "I'm going to make everyone proud." → AA +1, MC +1
- B) "I'm terrified and I don't know if I can do this." → MH +1 (vulnerability as strength)
- C) She doesn't think. She watches the water and lets the city sounds wash over her. → SD +1

**Scene 1.7 — The Night Before (No choice)**
Amelia lies in bed. She can hear her parents talking quietly downstairs. Her phone lights up — a text from Ella: "you're going to be amazing. don't forget me x." She stares at the ceiling. Sleep doesn't come easily.

**Chapter 1 Total Available Points:** AA 3, SI 1, MH 3, SD 4, MC 2, OK 2 = 15 (player can earn ~8 from 6 choices)

## CHAPTER 2: THE CALL TO ADVENTURE
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The summons to leave the ordinary world.
**Alchemical Stage:** Early Nigredo — the initial dissolution; leaving the vessel.
**Month:** Early October. **Location:** London → Plymouth.
**Mood:** Anxious excitement. Motion. The world getting bigger. First sight of the sea.

### Act 1: The Departure
**Scene 2.1 — Morning of the Move**
The car is packed. Grace is fussing. David is loading boxes with military precision. Lily is pretending she's fine.

**Choice 1:** Amelia's goodbye to Lily:
- A) "Look after mum and dad for me, yeah?" → SI +1 (Lily relationship +1)
- B) "You can call me anytime. About anything. I mean it." → MC +1 (Lily relationship +1)

**Scene 2.2 — The Drive**
Four hours in the car with David. The M5 unrolling. Conversation drifts between silence and small talk. David is not good at big emotions, but he tries.

**Conditional:** If Amelia said "I want to be the first with a degree" in Ch1, David says: "You know we're proud of you whatever happens, right? Degree or no degree."

**Scene 2.3 — First Sight of Plymouth**
They crest a hill and the sea appears. Plymouth spread out below, grey and blue and vast. Amelia's breath catches. This is real.

### Act 2: The Arrival
**Scene 2.4 — Halls of Residence**
The corridor. Other students moving in, parents carrying boxes, awkward hellos. Amelia's room: small, institutional, a single bed, a desk, and a window that looks out onto a car park (not the sea). She sits on the bed and thinks: *This is it.*

**Scene 2.5 — Meeting Liz**
Liz bursts in from the next room — she heard Amelia arrive through the wall. Immediate, enthusiastic, slightly overwhelming. Liz talks about marine biology, Cardiff, and her dog in the same sentence.

**Choice 2:** Amelia's response to Liz's energy:
- A) Matches it — "Oh my god, tell me everything about the dog" → SI +1 (Liz relationship +1)
- B) Polite but reserved — "It's nice to meet you too" → MH +1 (protecting energy)

**Scene 2.6 — The Kitchen**
Evening. The communal kitchen. People filtering in. This is where Amelia first sees:
- **Lucas** — sitting at the table with headphones on, eating cereal, reading. He nods at her. That's it.
- **Raj** — already cooking something that smells incredible. "Hungry? There's always enough."
- **Sarah** — sitting in the corner with a cup of tea, not talking to anyone. She looks up when Amelia enters. Their eyes meet. Sarah almost smiles, then looks away.

**Choice 3:** Who does Amelia approach first?
- A) Raj — the food smells amazing → SI +1 (Raj relationship +1)
- B) Lucas — curious about what he's reading → SD +1 (Lucas relationship +1)
- C) Sarah — something about her pulls Amelia over → MH +1 (Sarah relationship +1)

### Act 3: Orientation
**Scene 2.7 — Freshers' Week**
A montage of orientation events. Registration, campus tours, the psychology department welcome talk. Amelia is overwhelmed.

**Scene 2.8 — The SU Night Out**
First student night out. Loud music, cheap drinks, neon wristbands. Amelia is out of her comfort zone.

**Choice 4:** How does Amelia navigate the night?
- A) Throws herself in — dances, meets people, stays till close → SI +1
- B) Finds a quiet corner and people-watches — observes, takes it in → SD +1
- C) Leaves early, calls Ella from outside → MH +1 (Ella relationship +1, but SI opportunity missed)

**Scene 2.9 — The Psychology Welcome Lecture**
First real lecture. Prof. Hawthorne at the podium. He opens with: "Psychology is the study of behaviour and mind. It is also, if you are paying attention, the study of everything you are afraid to look at in yourself."

**Choice 5:** Amelia's reaction to Hawthorne:
- A) *Intimidating, but I want to impress him.* → AA +1
- B) *He's right. That's exactly why I'm here.* → SD +1
- C) *"Everything you're afraid to look at"... That's either brilliant or terrifying.* → OK +1

**Conditional Scene (SI ≥ 3 or Liz relationship ≥ 1):**
Liz drags Amelia to a flat party. Amelia meets Maya for the first time — she's reading tarot cards in the kitchen, half-seriously, half-joking. "Want to know your fortune? ... Interesting. The Tower. Don't worry — it's actually a good card. Eventually."
- If this scene triggers: Maya relationship +1, OK +1

**Chapter 2 Total Available Points:** AA 1, SI 3, MH 3, SD 3, MC 1, OK 2 = 13 (player earns ~8 from 5–6 choices)

## CHAPTER 3: REFUSAL OF THE CALL
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero resists the adventure; doubt and fear.
**Alchemical Stage:** Nigredo deepening — the first real dissolution; the old self crumbling.
**Month:** October–November. **Location:** Plymouth campus.
**Mood:** Grey, rainy, claustrophobic. Homesickness. Doubt. The first real taste of difficulty.

### Act 1: The Weight (Weeks 1–3)
- Amelia struggles with the workload. First essays. The reading list is enormous.
- Liz gets homesick — cries in the kitchen at 2am. Amelia comforts her (or doesn't).
- First contact with Tasha: a casual, cutting remark about Amelia's accent/background.

**Key Choices:**
- Comfort Liz vs. go back to bed → SI/MH
- Respond to Tasha vs. walk away → MC/MH

### Act 2: Cracks and Connections (Weeks 4–6)
- Zara introduced properly — she confronts Tasha's racism publicly in a seminar. Amelia witnesses it.
- Lucas invites Amelia to a Jung reading group he's started. It's just three people in his room, but the conversation is electric.
- Maya invites Amelia to a "meditation thing" which turns out to be a candlelit ceremony in Maya's room with incense and chanting. It's either moving or uncomfortable, depending on Amelia's disposition.
- Midterms approach. Amelia has her first panic attack in the library.

**Key Choices:**
- Support Zara publicly vs. stay quiet → MC/SI vs. personal safety
- Attend Jung group → SD +1, Lucas relationship +1
- Attend Maya's ceremony → OK +1, Maya relationship +1 **(CRITICAL: Second Elena requirement)**
- During panic attack: seek help vs. push through vs. call Ella → MH/AA/SI
- **Negative point opportunity:** If Amelia joins Tasha's group in mocking someone → MC -2, SI -1

### Act 3: The Pit (Weeks 7–9)
- The rain hasn't stopped for a week. Amelia is behind on essays. She hasn't called Ella in a while.
- Sarah appears again — they share a bench at Plymouth Hoe, looking at the sea. Sarah says something quietly devastating: "Do you ever feel like you're watching your life from outside? Like you're not really in it?" Amelia can respond with empathy, or deflection.
- Sophia appears — top of the class in the first assessments. Amelia feels inadequate.
- Prof. Hawthorne hands back essays with cutting but fair feedback.

**Key Choices:**
- Response to Sarah's vulnerability → MH/SI (Sarah relationship critical point)
- React to Sophia: compete vs. befriend vs. ignore → AA/SI
- Engage with Hawthorne's feedback: grow or shrink → AA/SD

**End of Chapter 3: Mentor Assignment Calculation triggers.**

**Chapter 3 Total Available Points:** AA 3, SI 3, MH 3, SD 2, MC 3, OK 2 = 16 (player earns ~10)

## CHAPTER 4: MEETING THE MENTOR
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero encounters a guide who prepares them for the journey.
**Alchemical Stage:** Late Nigredo / early Albedo — purification begins; a guide appears in the darkness.
**Month:** November. **Location:** Plymouth + first Cornwall trip.
**Mood:** Clarifying. The fog begins to lift — not because things get easier, but because someone is holding a lamp.

### Structure
This chapter has **four variant paths** based on mentor assignment. The core emotional beats are the same; the flavour, dialogue, and Cornwall destination differ.

### Core Events (All Paths)
1. **The Assignment** — Amelia is paired with her mentor through the department's "academic advisor" system. If Elena, it happens differently — Elena approaches Amelia directly, casually, with a comment about the book Amelia bought in Bromley.
2. **First Meeting** — A conversation that changes how Amelia sees the world. Each mentor offers a different lens.
3. **The Cornwall Trip** — Each mentor takes Amelia to Cornwall for different reasons.
4. **The Test** — Each mentor poses a challenge that reveals character.
5. **Group dynamics continue** — friend relationships deepen alongside the mentor arc.

### Hawthorne Path (AA+MC highest)
- First meeting: his office. He challenges her to defend her first essay position from the opposite side. She struggles but grows.
- Cornwall: Bodmin Moor — historical research sites, tin mining heritage. "This is where empires were built on other people's backs. Psychology without history is decoration."
- Test: He gives her access to a student's anonymised file and asks what she observes. It's clearly Sarah's profile. Does she break confidence? (MC test)

### Simmons Path (MH+SI highest)
- First meeting: her office (the plants, the biscuits, the comfortable chairs). Simmons asks: "How are you? And I mean actually — not the version you tell your parents."
- Cornwall: Eden Project + Lost Gardens of Heligan. "Restoration isn't going back. It's growing forward from what survived."
- Test: A fellow student breaks down in tears in the corridor. Simmons watches to see what Amelia does before intervening.

### Maya Path (SD+OK highest)
- First meeting: not an office — Maya takes Amelia for a walk at dawn along the Hoe. They watch the sun come up. Maya talks about attention as the root of all understanding.
- Cornwall: Tintagel + Mên-an-Tol. Myth as psychology, landscape as teacher. "You're standing where people have stood for five thousand years asking the same questions you're asking."
- Test: Maya asks Amelia to sit in silence for an hour at a sacred site and report what she noticed. What did she really see?

### Elena Path (OK ≥ 8, hidden)
- The approach is different. Elena doesn't have an office. She finds Amelia in the second-hand bookshop on the Barbican and makes a comment about the Paracelsus text: "Have you found the note yet? Really read it?"
- Cornwall: Madron Holy Well + Carn Euny Fogou. No explanation. "Just come." Elena walks fast and doesn't slow down.
- Test: Elena takes Amelia to the Merry Maidens at dusk and tells her a droll (Cornish folk tale) that seems to be about Amelia's life. "Now. What did the story tell you that I didn't?"

**Chapter 4 Total Available Points:** AA 2, SI 1, MH 1, SD 3, MC 1, OK 2 = 10 (player earns ~7)

## CHAPTER 5: CROSSING THE THRESHOLD
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero commits to the adventure and enters the unfamiliar world.
**Alchemical Stage:** Albedo — purification, separation of elements, gaining clarity.
**Month:** November–December. **Location:** Plymouth.
**Mood:** Settling in. Amelia is starting to belong. But belonging means you now have something to lose.

### Core Events
1. **Academic rhythm established** — studying, seminars, study groups. Amelia finds her groove.
2. **Michael introduced** — campus protest about cuts to mental health services. He gives a speech that electrifies. Amelia is drawn to his conviction; some friends find him intense.
3. **Sophia introduced properly** — the rivalry crystallises. They're assigned to the same project group.
4. **Raj's family pressure** — his mum calls during a group dinner; the conversation in Gujarati is tense. He's smiling when he hangs up. "It's fine. Family, innit." It's not fine.
5. **A random event** — the first Karma Dice roll. Something unexpected happens to one of Amelia's friends, and her response matters.
6. **Cornwall weekend** — the group goes together (non-mentor trip). Eden Project or coastline hike. Fun, bonding, a moment of genuine joy.

**Key Choices:**
- Michael's protest: join vs observe vs avoid → MC/SI/MH
- Sophia project: compete vs collaborate → AA/SI
- Push Raj about his family → SI (Raj relationship, risky) vs respect his privacy → MH
- Random event response → various stats based on dice outcome

**Chapter 5 Total Available Points:** AA 2, SI 2, MH 2, SD 1, MC 1, OK 2 = 10

## CHAPTER 6: TESTS, ALLIES, AND ENEMIES
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero faces challenges, identifies friends and foes.
**Alchemical Stage:** Albedo continued — separation of the pure from the impure.
**Month:** December–January. **Location:** Plymouth + Christmas break.
**Mood:** Tension rising. Conflicts come to a head. Alliances solidify. Christmas provides a brief respite but doesn't resolve anything.

### Core Events
1. **Tasha escalation** — she targets Zara with a sustained campaign. It crosses from personal to institutional (social media, graffiti, a formal complaint that's actually gaslighting). Amelia must choose sides explicitly.
2. **Sarah withdrawing** — she's attending fewer classes. Her texts are shorter. She cancelled plans three times. The signs are there for anyone paying attention.
3. **Lucas vulnerability** — he opens up about his father for the first time. A 3am kitchen conversation that can go very deep or stay surface-level.
4. **Academic pressure** — Christmas exams. Hawthorne (or respective mentor) raises the bar.
5. **Christmas break** — a brief return to London. Family warmth. But Amelia is different now, and everyone can feel it.
6. **The Occult Thread** — if Amelia is on the OK path, she finds a reference in an old text to a "Cornish working" that can only be performed at midwinter. Elena (if mentor) invites her. Maya (if not Elena path) mentions it obliquely.

**Key Choices:**
- Tasha/Zara conflict: confront, report, ignore → MC/SI combinations
- Sarah check-in: push gently, give space, mention to a professional → MH/MC/SI (Sarah relationship critical)
- Lucas 3am conversation: listen deeply vs deflect with humour → SI/SD (Lucas relationship)
- Christmas: talk to parents honestly vs perform "everything's fine" → MH/SI
- Midwinter occult option (if available) → OK +2

**Chapter 6 Total Available Points:** AA 1, SI 3, MH 2, SD 1, MC 3, OK 2 = 12

## CHAPTER 7: THE APPROACH
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero prepares for the central challenge.
**Alchemical Stage:** Late Albedo / Citrinitas beginning — the yellowing; insight dawns, but the fire approaches.
**Month:** January–February. **Location:** Plymouth.
**Mood:** Gathering storm. Everything is intensifying. The days are short and dark. Something is coming.

### Core Events
1. **The mentor deepens** — the second significant mentor scene. More personal, more challenging. The mentor reveals something about themselves.
2. **Sarah's darkness visible** — Amelia (if paying attention) catches a glimpse of Sarah's self-harm, or a poem Sarah wrote, or an overheard conversation. The crisis is approaching.
3. **Amelia's own shadow** — she snaps at someone she cares about. Or she does something selfish. The Shadow (in Jung's terms) emerges. She doesn't like what she sees.
4. **Sophia pivot point** — Sophia fails a test for the first time. Her reaction reveals vulnerability. Door opens for friendship.
5. **Research ethics dilemma** — an academic situation where the "right" thing to do isn't clear. Mentor-dependent framing.
6. **Preparation** — whether she knows it or not, Amelia is being prepared for the ordeal.

**Key Choices:**
- Respond to mentor's vulnerability: reciprocate or deflect → SD/SI
- Sarah intervention timing: act now vs wait vs deny what you're seeing → MH/MC/SI (Sarah score critical)
- Own shadow: acknowledge it vs suppress it → SD/MH
- Sophia: reach out or let her struggle alone → SI/AA
- Research dilemma: integrity vs expedience → MC/AA

**Chapter 7 Total Available Points:** AA 2, SI 1, MH 2, SD 2, MC 1, OK 2 = 10

## CHAPTER 8: THE ORDEAL
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The supreme crisis — death and rebirth.
**Alchemical Stage:** Citrinitas — the yellowing; burning in the furnace; the ego breaks.
**Month:** February. **Location:** Plymouth.
**Mood:** **Dark.** This is the hardest chapter. The rain doesn't stop. Everything comes to a head.

### CONTENT WARNING
This chapter deals directly with depression, self-harm, suicidal ideation, and (potentially) suicide. In-game, this will be preceded by a content warning and the option to view a summary instead of the full scene. Helpline information will be displayed.

### Act 1: The Cracks Widen
- Amelia's own mental health is fraying. She's not sleeping. She missed a deadline. Her inner monologue is harsh — her own worst critic.
- Academic crisis: failed exam, or thesis rejection, or plagiarism accusation (varies by mentor path and AA score).
- Tasha does something genuinely harmful — not just bullying but something with consequences.

### Act 2: Sarah's Crisis
- Sarah stops responding to everyone.
- A day passes. Then two.
- Amelia finds Sarah — or doesn't, depending on timing and the Sarah Score calculation.
- **This is the scene.** It is written with care and respect. It is not a plot twist; it is the reality that some people's pain becomes unbearable.

**The Sarah Score determines outcome (see game_mechanics.md):**
- Full save: Amelia gets there, says the right thing not because she has a script but because she *has been that person who shows up*. Professional help is called.
- Late save: Amelia almost misses it. Sarah survives. It's close.
- Partial save: Sarah survives but is transferred away. A letter arrives later.
- Tragic: Sarah is gone. The game marks this permanently.

### Act 3: In the Ashes
- If Sarah survives: hospital visit, fragile conversations, the beginning of real recovery
- If Sarah dies: memorial, grief, guilt, the question *could I have done more?*
- In either case: Amelia faces herself. Her Shadow is no longer theoretical. She must decide who she is going to be.
- Mentor scene: the mentor meets Amelia in the aftermath. Each mentor handles it differently:
  - Hawthorne: "Grief is not a problem to be solved. It is a process to be endured."
  - Simmons: [sits with her in silence for a long time before speaking]
  - Maya: Takes her to a place of natural beauty and says nothing for an hour.
  - Elena: "The fire doesn't ask permission. You're in it now. So breathe."

**Karma Dice: Triggered for the Sarah intervention timing even within the "save" tier.**

**Key Choices:**
- How Amelia handles her own crisis → MH/AA
- Critical Sarah intervention choices → various (but mostly determined by accumulated score)
- Tasha aftermath: forgive, fight, or freeze → MC/SI
- Accept help from friends or retreat into isolation → MH/SI
- **Negative points available:** Ignoring Sarah's crisis completely → MH -2, SI -1, MC -2

**Chapter 8 Total Available Points:** AA 1, SI 2, MH 3, SD 1, MC 3, OK 2 = 12

## CHAPTER 9: THE REWARD
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero gains something — knowledge, treasure, understanding.
**Alchemical Stage:** Late Citrinitas — the gold begins to emerge from the fire.
**Month:** March. **Location:** Plymouth + Cornwall.
**Mood:** Raw but hopeful. Spring is coming. Things are not fixed, but they are *different*.

### Core Events
1. **Academic rebound** — Amelia's work improves, deepened by real experience. Her essays have something they didn't before: lived truth.
2. **Friendship consolidation** — the friend group is closer for having survived the crisis. Or changed, if Sarah was lost.
3. **Sarah's status** — if alive: fragile recovery scenes, honest conversations. If dead: the group grieves together; absence is presence.
4. **Personal growth markers** — Amelia surprises herself: she stands up to someone, helps a stranger, falls in love with her field again.
5. **Cornwall reward** — a trip that is restorative rather than testing. The landscape heals.
6. **Mentor acknowledgment** — the mentor recognises Amelia's transformation.

**Key Choices:**
- How to process the ordeal: grow or calcify → SD/MH
- Support the friend group or lean on them → SI
- Academic recovery: honest work or shortcuts → AA/MC
- Cornwall engagement → SD/OK

**Chapter 9 Total Available Points:** AA 2, SI 2, MH 2, SD 2, MC 1, OK 1 = 10

## CHAPTER 11: THE RESURRECTION
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero faces a final test that synthesises all growth.
**Alchemical Stage:** Rubedo — the completed Work; the stone is formed (or not).
**Month:** May. **Location:** Plymouth + Cornwall (final trip).
**Mood:** Luminous. The days are long. Everything has a heightened quality — last lectures, last nights out, last conversations before summer scatters everyone.

### Core Events
1. **Final exams** — the culmination of the academic year. Not just a test of knowledge but of everything Amelia has become.
2. **Sophia alliance** — if the relationship was built, Sophia becomes a study partner and friend. If not, the rivalry has one final flare.
3. **Tasha resolution** — if compassion path: Tasha's vulnerability scene. If anger path: Tasha departs. If avoidance: Tasha remains unresolved shadow.
4. **End-of-year celebration** — the group together, possibly for the last time in this configuration. Raj cooks something enormous. Maya brings candles. Lucas makes a toast so quiet everyone has to lean in.
5. **Cornwall: The Final Trip** — this varies dramatically by path:
   - Academic path: Hawthorne takes Amelia to the library at Exeter for a special viewing of historical texts
   - Healer path: Simmons leads a group wellness retreat at the Eden Project
   - Seeker path: Maya takes Amelia to a dawn meditation at a stone circle
   - Elena path: **The Fogou.** Elena takes Amelia to Carn Euny. Amelia enters the underground chamber alone by candlelight. What happens inside is the game's most mysterious scene.
6. **The resurrection test** — each path has a final inner challenge. Karma Dice rolls here determine the specific nature of what Amelia faces.

### The Fogou Scene (Elena/OK Path)
This is the hidden crown jewel of the game. Amelia crawls into the fogou alone. Elena waits outside. Inside, by candlelight, Amelia experiences... something. Depending on her OK score and SD score, this ranges from:
- A profound silence that reveals her own depth (OK moderate)
- Visions connecting her journey to the alchemical stages (OK high)
- An encounter with something — someone? — that is either a hallucination, a memory, or genuinely otherworldly (OK exceptional)

This scene is written ambiguously. The game never confirms whether the supernatural is real. The player must decide.

**Chapter 11 Total Available Points:** AA 2, SI 1, MH 2, SD 3, MC 2, OK 2 = 12

## CHAPTER 12: RETURN WITH THE ELIXIR
**Hero's Journey Stage:** The hero returns home transformed, bearing a gift for the world.
**Alchemical Stage:** Post-Rubedo — the Work is done; the gold is real; the journey begins again.
**Month:** June. **Location:** Plymouth → London.
**Mood:** Varies by ending, but all share a sense of *completion and beginning*.

### Structure
Chapter 12 branches into seven endings based on the final calculation (see game_mechanics.md). Each ending has:
1. A **shared opening** — the last morning in Plymouth, packing up the room, final goodbyes
2. A **unique final sequence** — 3–5 scenes specific to the ending
3. A **closing image** — the last thing the player sees
4. **Credits** with ending-specific music

### Shared Opening
Amelia wakes up in her room for the last time. The walls are bare. Everything is in boxes again — but different boxes, and she's a different person packing them. She walks through campus one last time. The Hoe. The library. The kitchen where it all began.

**Endings (see game_mechanics.md for full calculation):**

### 1. The Scholar

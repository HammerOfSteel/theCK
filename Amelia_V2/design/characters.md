# The CK: Amelia V2 — Character Bible

> Every character in this story represents both a real person Amelia might meet at university and an archetypal force in her psychological/alchemical transformation. The surface must always feel real — the archetype lives underneath, never spoken aloud.

---

## AMELIA JAMES — The Protagonist

### The Basics
- **Age:** 18 (turns 19 in spring)
- **From:** Suburban London (Bromley area)
- **Course:** BSc Psychology, University of Plymouth
- **Appearance:** Brown eyes, long wavy brown hair she's always pushing behind her ears. Fair skin, average height. Dresses in layers — cardigans over band tees, Converse, a corduroy jacket she got from a charity shop. Carries a tote bag with too many books in it.

### Personality
- **Curious and earnest** — genuinely excited about learning, sometimes embarrassingly so
- **Conflict-avoidant** — her biggest flaw; she'd rather absorb someone's cruelty than confront it
- **Quietly perceptive** — notices things about people others miss, but doesn't always trust her instincts
- **Anxious overachiever** — studies too hard, sleeps too little, eats irregularly when stressed
- **Dry humour** — not the loudest person in the room, but when she speaks, it lands
- **Deeply empathetic** — feels other people's pain almost physically, which is both her gift and her burden

### Internal Arc (The Alchemical Reading)
Amelia's journey maps to the **Magnum Opus**:
- **Nigredo** (Blackening) — Chapters 1–3: dissolution of her old identity; leaving home, losing certainty
- **Albedo** (Whitening) — Chapters 4–7: purification through reflection; mentorship, study, Cornwall
- **Citrinitas** (Yellowing) — Chapters 8–9: the ordeal and its reward; confronting the Shadow, Sarah's crisis
- **Rubedo** (Reddening) — Chapters 10–12: integration and rebirth; coming home as a changed person

### What She Wants vs What She Needs
- **Wants:** To succeed academically, to be liked, to understand why people suffer
- **Needs:** To learn that understanding comes through *experiencing* — not just studying; that she cannot save everyone; that her own shadow must be faced before she can help others

### Voice Sample
> "I keep reading about individuation but I can't even individuate myself from my mum's cooking. She sent me a Tupperware of jerk chicken through the post. Through the *post*, Ella."

## ELLA CHEN — The Anchor

### The Basics
- **Age:** 18
- **From:** Same part of London as Amelia
- **Course:** English Literature, staying in London (Queen Mary)
- **Appearance:** Short curly blonde hair (dyed — naturally dark), blue eyes, petite. Always in oversized jumpers and silver rings on every finger.

### Personality
- **Warm, loud, loyal** — the kind of friend who answers the phone at 3am and doesn't complain about it
- **Emotionally intelligent** — reads people like novels
- **A bit chaotic** — messy room, seventeen half-finished projects, always late
- **Grounding force** — calls Amelia on her bullshit in the kindest possible way
- **Insecurity she hides** — worries she's not smart enough for her course; covers it with jokes

### Role in the Story
Ella is Amelia's connection to who she was before Plymouth. She appears primarily through phone calls, texts, and during the London-return chapters. Her distance means the player must *choose* to maintain the friendship — SI points are partly about not letting Ella drift away.

### Arc
- Early: Ella is the safety net — every time Amelia panics, she calls Ella
- Mid: Ella starts pulling away slightly (her own life is happening) — Amelia must learn to cope alone
- Late: The friendship is either deeper for having survived distance, or frayed by neglect
- In the Cornish-folklore reading, Ella is the **golden thread** — the connection to the ordinary world that lets the hero return

### Voice Sample
> "Okay, first of all, breathe. Second of all, I'm googling 'how to fight a bully through a laptop screen.' Third of all, did you eat today? Amelia. *Did you eat today.*"

## PROF. ARTHUR HAWTHORNE — The Rational Mentor

### The Basics
- **Age:** 58
- **From:** Originally from Bath, lived in Plymouth for 25 years
- **Role:** Head of Psychology Department, specialises in research methodology and history of psychology
- **Appearance:** Greying hair, neatly trimmed beard, wire-rimmed glasses. Always in a tweed jacket with elbow patches (he knows it's a cliché; he doesn't care). Tall, slightly stooped from years of reading. Office smells of old paper and Earl Grey.

### Personality
- **Intellectually rigorous** — demands precision in thinking and writing
- **Dry, understated wit** — his deadpan delivery makes students unsure if he's joking
- **Patient but exacting** — will wait for you to find the answer yourself, then tell you why it's not quite right
- **Secretly passionate** — beneath the academic reserve is someone who genuinely believes knowledge can save the world
- **Old-fashioned integrity** — will not bend rules, even when it would be kinder to

### Role as Mentor (Unlocked by high AA + MC)
Hawthorne teaches Amelia that **truth requires discipline**. His Cornwall trip takes her to the Bodleian connections at Exeter Cathedral and to Plymouth's own naval history — the empirical world, the provable. He represents the alchemical principle of **Salt** — the material, the structured, the body of knowledge.

### Arc
- Initially intimidating — Amelia thinks he doesn't like her
- Gradually reveals warmth and genuine investment in her growth
- His "test" involves an ethical dilemma: does she report academic misconduct even when it costs her?
- If Amelia chooses him, he eventually shares that he once faced a similar crisis in his own career — chose integrity and paid for it

### Voice Sample
> "Miss James, I didn't ask what you *feel* about Milgram's experiment. I asked what it *demonstrates*. Your feelings are valid. They are also not the assignment."

## DR. NADIA SIMMONS — The Compassionate Mentor

### The Basics
- **Age:** 38
- **From:** Birmingham, Caribbean heritage (Trinidadian parents)
- **Role:** Lecturer in Positive Psychology and Wellbeing
- **Appearance:** Warm brown skin, natural hair usually in a wrap or braids, bright colours in her clothing. Radiates energy. Her office has plants everywhere and a sign that says "You Are Not Your GPA."

### Personality
- **Genuinely optimistic** — but not naïvely so; she has earned her optimism through personal struggle
- **Approachable** — the professor whose office hours are always overbooked
- **Boundary-conscious** — will support you but won't do your work for you
- **Subtly political** — uses her position to advocate for mental health services and BAME students
- **Imperfect** — sometimes her positivity can feel dismissive to people in real pain; she's working on this

### Role as Mentor (Unlocked by high MH + SI)
Simmons teaches Amelia that **healing is an active practice**. Her Cornwall trip takes Amelia to the Eden Project and the Lost Gardens of Heligan — places of restoration and regrowth. She represents the alchemical principle of **Mercury** — the fluid, the adaptive, the force of connection and communication.

### Arc
- Amelia initially assumes she's too "soft" for serious academia
- Simmons demonstrates that compassion *is* rigorous — it requires as much discipline as empiricism
- Her "test" involves Amelia choosing between her own wellbeing and helping a friend (Sarah)
- She reveals her own history with depression — she knows the territory from the inside

### Voice Sample
> "Look, I could cite you twelve studies on resilience and positive reframing. But right now? Right now I think you need a cup of tea and to cry for a bit. Come on. I have biscuits."

## MAYA PATEL — The Mystic Friend / Spiritual Mentor

### The Basics
- **Age:** 20 (second year)
- **From:** Bristol, Indian-British heritage
- **Course:** Philosophy with Psychology joint honours
- **Appearance:** Olive skin, long curly black hair usually loose or in a messy braid. Bohemian — flowy skirts, embroidered jackets, always smells of sandalwood or patchouli. Silver crescent moon necklace. Henna on her hands sometimes.

### Personality
- **Genuinely seeking** — not performing spirituality; she is on a real quest for meaning
- **Intellectually voracious** — reads Alan Watts, Dogen, Paracelsus, and Reddit conspiracy theories with equal enthusiasm
- **Eccentric** — makes her own tea blends, reads tarot, wakes up at 5am to meditate, keeps a dream journal
- **Prone to grandiosity** — sometimes mistakes intensity for depth; she's still young
- **Deeply kind** — her spirituality is rooted in compassion, not escapism
- **The bridge** — she connects the academic world to the esoteric one naturally

### Role as Mentor (Unlocked by high SD + OK)
Maya teaches Amelia that **there are ways of knowing beyond the rational**. Her Cornwall connection is deep — she has been visiting sacred sites since she was a teenager. She takes Amelia to Mên-an-Tol, Madron Holy Well, and the stone circles of Bodmin Moor. She represents the alchemical principle of **Sulphur** — the volatile, the spiritual, the animating fire.

### Arc
- Amelia is drawn to Maya's certainty and warmth
- Mid-story: Maya's own spiritual practice is tested — she has a crisis of faith when bad things happen to good people (Sarah)
- Late: Maya either deepens into genuine wisdom or retreats into spiritual bypassing, depending on how Amelia engages with her
- Maya is the gateway to the occult path, but she is not the final guide — that role belongs to Elena

### Voice Sample
> "Okay, hear me out — what if Jung was essentially describing the same process the alchemists were, just without the mercury and the furnaces? Like, the *prima materia* is the unconscious. The gold is individuation. It's the same map, Amelia, just different cartographers."

## LUCAS ADEYEMI — The Quiet Thinker

### The Basics
- **Age:** 19
- **From:** Leeds, Nigerian-British heritage
- **Course:** Psychology
- **Appearance:** Light brown skin, short black hair kept neat, angular face, slim build. Dresses simply — dark colours, clean lines. Always has headphones around his neck. Quiet eyes that miss nothing.

### Personality
- **Introverted but not shy** — he's comfortable being quiet; he just doesn't see the point of talking for the sake of it
- **Deeply thoughtful** — when he speaks, people listen because he's been thinking for twenty minutes before opening his mouth
- **Sardonic humour** — delivered so deadpan people sometimes miss that he's funny
- **Loyal once trust is earned** — slow to warm up but unshakeable once he does
- **Emotionally guarded** — has his own wounds he doesn't talk about (absent father, pressure from mother to succeed)
- **Jungian fascination** — reads Jung obsessively, which is how he and Amelia bond

### Role in the Story
Lucas is Amelia's main intellectual companion at Plymouth. He's the person she argues ideas with at 1am in the kitchen. Their relationship can develop into deep friendship or (if the player steers it) something more ambiguous — but this is *not* a romance game; it's about the tension of intimacy in friendship.

### Arc
- Starts closed-off — polite but distant
- Bond over shared Jung reading group
- Mid-story: Amelia learns about his family situation; he learns to let someone in
- His crisis: he either confronts his own Shadow (his anger at his father) or stays locked in intellectualisation
- In the Jungian reading, Lucas represents the **Animus** — the contra-sexual archetype, the intellectual masculine that Amelia must integrate

### Voice Sample
> "You know what I think about at 3am? Not the exam. The exam is just... the exam. I think about whether my dad ever thought about me at 3am. And then I think about Jung saying 'the most terrifying thing is to accept oneself completely.' And then I make toast."

## ZARA OKAFOR — The Fighter

### The Basics
- **Age:** 20 (second year, retaking some first-year modules)
- **From:** London (Lewisham), born in Nigeria, moved to UK at age 7
- **Course:** Psychology with Criminology
- **Appearance:** Dark skin, long black hair in protective styles (braids, locs, twists — changes throughout the year). Strong features, warm smile that can turn to steel in a second. Fit — she boxes at the university gym.

### Personality
- **Direct and unapologetic** — says what she means, doesn't code-switch for comfort
- **Fiery but fair** — will call out racism forcefully but doesn't enjoy conflict for its own sake
- **Resilient** — has survived things that would have broken most people; she's still standing
- **Good at reading rooms** — knows exactly how power dynamics work and who has it
- **Protective** — takes Amelia under her wing partly because she sees herself a year ago
- **Vulnerable underneath** — the armour is real, but it costs her; she sometimes pushes people away before they can hurt her

### Role in the Story  
Zara is Amelia's introduction to the reality of racism and systemic injustice at university. She forces Amelia to move beyond abstract knowledge into lived experience. She also provides a crucial contrast — Zara has already been through her own Nigredo; she's further along in the Work than Amelia.

### Arc
- First meeting involves Zara defending herself against Tasha's racism — Amelia must choose whether to intervene
- Their friendship grows through shared study sessions and late nights
- Mid-story: Zara's past trauma surfaces; she considers dropping out
- Late: depending on player choices, Amelia either helps Zara stay and fight, or watches her leave
- In the alchemical reading, Zara is the **Red Lion** — sulphurous, fiery, the principle of transformation through confrontation

### Voice Sample
> "You read about systemic racism in a textbook? That's cute. I'll tell you about systemic racism at 2am at a bus stop in Lewisham. But first — did you see Tasha's face when I told her I was retaking her module because I *chose* to switch courses? Priceless."

## RAJ SHARMA — The Heart

### The Basics
- **Age:** 21 (mature for his year — took a gap year working in his family's business)
- **From:** Manchester, Indian-British heritage (Gujarati family)
- **Course:** Psychology, specialising in family systems and developmental psychology
- **Appearance:** Dark skin, short curly black hair, warm brown eyes, broad shoulders. Dresses casually — hoodies, jeans, trainers. Always has food on him; his mum sends care packages every two weeks.

### Personality
- **The group's emotional centre** — warm, generous, effortlessly likeable
- **Conflict mediator** — the person everyone goes to when tensions arise
- **Hides complexity behind affability** — people assume he's simple because he's kind; he's not
- **Family-oriented** — his psychology interest stems from navigating a large, complicated family
- **Insecure about intelligence** — he's actually very smart but compares himself to academic types like Lucas and Sophia
- **Food is love** — expresses care through cooking; his flat kitchen is the social hub

### Role in the Story
Raj grounds the friend group in warmth and normalcy. He's the one who organises things — the pub quiz, the group study session, the "let's actually go outside for once" hike. He represents the importance of community and chosen family.

### Arc
- Immediate warmth — Amelia likes him instantly
- Mid-story: His family pressures him to abandon psychology for business; he must choose
- His crisis is about authenticity vs. obligation — following his own path or the one his family expects
- In the Jungian reading, Raj represents the **Self in community** — the archetype of belonging, of the village, of home recreated away from home

### Voice Sample
> "Okay, listen, my nan makes the best dal in Manchester. In the *north of England*. I'm not saying my cooking is as good. I'm saying it's... a loving tribute. Now sit down and eat before Lucas philosophises us out of being hungry."

## SARAH WHITMORE — The Mirror

### The Basics
- **Age:** 18
- **From:** Rural Devon (small village outside Exeter)
- **Course:** Psychology
- **Appearance:** Pale skin, short blonde hair that she cuts herself (unevenly), blue eyes that look somewhere far away. Thin — she forgets to eat. Wears oversized clothes as if trying to disappear. A small tattoo of a wren on her inner wrist.

### Personality
- **Quiet and watchful** — takes in everything, gives little away
- **Bitterly funny when she opens up** — her humour is dark and surprising
- **Sensitive to beauty** — notices the light on water, the sound of rain, the shape of a cloud; she doesn't think she deserves to enjoy these things
- **Profound self-loathing** — depression tells her she is a burden; she believes it
- **Artistic** — writes poetry she never shows anyone; draws in the margins of her notes
- **Desperate for connection but terrified of it** — pushes people away then hates herself for being alone

### Role in the Story
Sarah is the story's emotional core and its most demanding moral challenge. She represents the question: *can you save someone who doesn't want to be saved — and should you try?* Her fate is the single most consequential branching point in the game.

### CRITICAL DESIGN NOTE: Handling with Care
Sarah's storyline deals with depression and suicidal ideation. The V2 treatment must:
- Never romanticise or aestheticise suicide
- Show that depression is an illness, not a character flaw or a choice
- Provide in-game resources (hotline numbers) in relevant scenes
- Make clear that Amelia cannot "fix" Sarah with the right choices — she can only *be there*, and even that might not be enough
- The tragic outcome (Sarah's death) should feel like a real loss, not a failure state or punishment

### Arc
- Amelia meets Sarah by chance — a quiet moment in the library, or a shared bench looking at the sea
- Their connection is immediate but fragile — Sarah opens up in glimpses, then retreats
- Mid-story: Sarah's depression worsens; she stops attending classes, stops answering texts
- The Ordeal (Chapter 8): Sarah's crisis. The player's accumulated MH, SI, MC, and emotional investment in Sarah determine the range of outcomes:
  - **Best case:** Sarah is hospitalised, begins recovery, survives — the friendship endures
  - **Middle case:** Sarah attempts; she survives but their friendship is damaged; long road ahead
  - **Worst case:** Sarah dies. This cannot be "undone." The rest of the story is different.
- In the alchemical reading, Sarah is the **Caput Mortuum** — the death's head, the residue of calcination. She is what remains when everything has been burned away. Amelia must learn whether the *prima materia* can be recovered from that ash.

### Voice Sample
> "You know that thing where the sea looks really calm and you think, that doesn't look cold at all? And then you put your foot in and it's so cold it burns? That's basically... I don't know. Never mind. Forget I said anything."

## ELENA TREVORRAN — The Hidden Guide

### The Basics
- **Age:** 45
- **From:** Penzance, Cornwall (Cornish family going back generations)
- **Role:** Mature student in the Psychology programme — or so it appears
- **Appearance:** Long dark hair with a single streak of silver, weathered hands, green eyes. Wears practical clothing — boots, waxed jackets, wool. A silver ring with the Cornish chough. Smells of woodsmoke and wild herbs.

### True Nature (Revealed Gradually)
Elena is a **pellar** — a Cornish cunning woman, heir to a folk tradition of herbalism, divination, and what the old drolls called *an gwella* (the seeing). She enrolled in the psychology programme partly out of genuine curiosity and partly because the university sits on land that was once significant. She is the hidden mentor — accessible *only* to players with very high OK scores and who have made specific choices involving Maya's spiritual path.

### Personality
- **Watchful and measured** — speaks only when she has something worth saying
- **Wry, earthy humour** — none of Maya's New Age aesthetics; Elena's spirituality is muddy boots and cold hands
- **Deeply knowledgeable** — about Cornish history, folklore, herbalism, and the esoteric tradition
- **Not mystified** — she treats the occult as practical knowledge, like carpentry or navigation
- **Challenging** — she doesn't give answers; she gives *tasks* and waits for you to find the answer yourself
- **Morally complex** — she has her own agenda; she may not always have Amelia's best interests at heart

### Role as Hidden Mentor (Unlocked by very high OK)
Elena's mentorship is the most demanding and the most transformative. She takes Amelia to the real Cornwall — not the tourist sites but the holy wells, the fogous (underground chambers), the Mên-an-Tol, the sites where the old practices still breathe. She teaches through story, through task, through silence. Her Cornwall represents the alchemical **Athanor** — the furnace in which the Great Work is performed.

### Arc
- First appearance: a quiet, slightly odd mature student who seems to know things she shouldn't
- Mid-story: starts dropping hints — a Cornish word here, a knowing look there — only visible to players on the OK path
- If unlocked: her mentorship is intense and disorienting; Amelia questions reality
- Elena's "test" is the most severe: Amelia must spend a night alone at a sacred site and face whatever comes
- Her revelation: the psychology programme and the alchemical tradition are studying the same thing with different instruments
- In the alchemical reading, Elena is the **Soror Mystica** — the mystical sister, the feminine guide through the Great Work

### Voice Sample
> "You want to understand the mind? Then stop reading about it in a library and come stand in the rain on a moor at midnight. The old ones didn't have textbooks. They had *this*." [gestures at the landscape] "And it was enough."

## TASHA REYNOLDS — The Shadow

### The Basics
- **Age:** 20 (second year)
- **From:** Surrey (wealthy family)
- **Course:** Psychology
- **Appearance:** Blonde, fair-skinned, conventionally attractive, always well-dressed. Perfect makeup, expensive bag, the kind of person who makes you feel underdressed just by existing.

### Personality
- **Insecure beneath the armour** — her cruelty comes from fear, not strength
- **Socially intelligent** — knows exactly how to humiliate someone without technically breaking any rules
- **Classist more than racist** — her bigotry is about superiority in all directions; race is one axis among many
- **Not a cartoon villain** — she has moments of genuine vulnerability; her home life is miserable (emotionally absent parents, conditional love based on performance)
- **Capable of change** — but only if someone has the patience and courage to see past her defences

### Role in the Story
Tasha is Amelia's **Shadow** in the Jungian sense — she embodies everything Amelia fears and represses: cruelty, competitiveness, the desire to dominate rather than connect. Confronting Tasha is not about defeating her; it's about understanding what she represents.

### Arc
- Introduction as antagonist — mocking, excluding, targeting Zara and Amelia
- Mid-story: escalation — she causes real harm (reported to university, affects someone's grades, triggers a crisis)
- Turning point: Amelia either confronts her with compassion (high MC + SI) or with anger (high MC alone) or avoids her entirely (low MC)
- If compassion: Tasha breaks down, begins to change — becomes an unlikely ally in the final act
- If anger: Tasha is defeated but not healed; she transfers or drops out; Amelia wins but feels hollow
- If avoidance: Tasha remains a toxic presence; the Shadow is not integrated

### Voice Sample
> [public] "Oh, love, is that a *charity shop* jacket? No, genuinely, I think that's sweet. Very... sustainable."
> [private, much later, if compassion path] "My mum hasn't called in three weeks. She knows about the disciplinary. She just... hasn't called."

## MICHAEL OKONKWO — The Activist

### The Basics
- **Age:** 22 (third year)
- **From:** London (Hackney)
- **Course:** Politics and Psychology joint honours
- **Appearance:** Tall, dark-skinned, commanding presence. Natural hair, usually in a small afro. Wears political t-shirts, always carrying flyers for something.

### Personality
- **Passionate and articulate** — can turn any conversation into a rally
- **Righteous** — sometimes to a fault; can be judgmental of people who aren't as politically engaged
- **Charismatic** — people follow him because he makes you feel like you can change the world
- **Blind spots** — focuses on systemic issues and sometimes misses the personal; can be insensitive to individual pain in pursuit of collective justice
- **Genuine** — his activism is not performance; he has skin in the game

### Role in the Story
Michael introduces the social justice dimension — protests, university politics, the structural issues behind individual suffering. He forces Amelia to see beyond her own experience. His challenge to her: *knowing is not enough; what will you actually do?*

### Voice Sample
> "You're studying *why* people suffer. I'm asking *who benefits from that suffering*. Those are different questions, Amelia, and only one of them changes anything."

## SOPHIA LANGFORD — The Rival

### The Basics
- **Age:** 19
- **From:** Oxford (academic family — both parents are professors)
- **Course:** Psychology
- **Appearance:** Red curly hair, freckles, intense green eyes. Well-spoken. Dresses smartly — somewhere between student and young professional.

### Personality
- **Brilliant and knows it** — competitive to a fault; treats university like a race she must win
- **Not malicious** — her competitiveness isn't personal; she genuinely doesn't understand why everyone isn't trying as hard as she is
- **Lonely** — being the smartest person in the room is isolating; she craves connection but doesn't know how
- **Fragile ego** — any perceived failure sends her spiralling; she has never learned to be bad at anything
- **Unexpected depth** — if Amelia gets past the surface, Sophia is thoughtful, funny, and desperately in need of a friend

### Role in the Story
Sophia is the academic foil — she pushes Amelia to be better, then forces her to ask *better at what?* Her rivalry can become friendship if Amelia invests in it.

### Arc
- Introduced as the person who always answers first in lectures, gets the highest marks, makes Amelia feel inadequate
- Mid-story: Sophia faces her first academic failure and doesn't know how to cope
- Late: becomes an ally if Amelia reaches out; remains a rival if ignored
- In the alchemical reading, Sophia is the **White Queen** — the purified intellect that must be married to the Red King (felt experience) to complete the work

### Voice Sample
> "I don't understand why you'd choose to go to a film when you could be reading the Kahneman. It's *Kahneman*. Films will always be there. This essay is due Thursday."
> [later, quietly] "...Do you want to get coffee? I don't actually have anyone to get coffee with."

## SUPPORTING CAST

### Mr. James (David) — Amelia's Father
- Age 46, works in IT, Jamaican heritage. Quiet, steady, expresses love through practical acts (fixing things, driving her to Plymouth). Worries about Amelia more than he shows.

### Mrs. James (Grace) — Amelia's Mother
- Age 44, teaching assistant, Jamaican heritage. Warm, talkative, sends Tupperware containers of food. More openly emotional than David; struggles to let go.

### Lily James — Amelia's Cousin
- Age 16, looks up to Amelia fiercely. Texts her constantly. Her own emerging identity questions (she's questioning her sexuality, hinted at but not central) give Amelia a mentoring role.

### Liz Torres — The Roommate
- Age 18, from Cardiff, studying Marine Biology. Olive skin, green eyes, short curly hair. Cheerful, messy, stays up too late watching nature documentaries. First friend Amelia makes by proximity. Gets homesick in week 3 and cries in the kitchen — a bonding moment.

| Amelia ↔ Zara | Found sisterhood | Amelia's naivety about race angers Zara initially | Can solidarity be learned, or must it be lived? |

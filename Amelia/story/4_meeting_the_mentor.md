# Chapter Overview
In this chapter, Amelia will meet one of three potential mentors who will guide her through her journey at Plymouth University. The mentor she meets will depend on her current point combination. Each mentor will offer unique insights, challenges, and opportunities for growth in different areas of Amelia's life.

# Mentor Selection

## Prof. Hawthorne (High AA and MC)
If Amelia has high Academic Achievement (AA) and Moral Choices (MC) points, she will be matched with Prof. Hawthorne, the head of the psychology department. Prof. Hawthorne will take a keen interest in Amelia's academic potential and guide her through the rigors of the psychology program. He will also challenge Amelia to consider the ethical implications of her studies and future career.

## Dr. Simmons (High MH and SI)
If Amelia has high Mental Health (MH) and Social Interaction (SI) points, she will be matched with Dr. Simmons, who teaches positive psychology. Dr. Simmons will help Amelia navigate the social and emotional challenges of university life, offering guidance on self-care, interpersonal relationships, and maintaining a positive outlook in the face of adversity.

## Maya (High SD and OK)
If Amelia has high Self-Discovery (SD) and Occult Knowledge (OK) points, she will be drawn to Maya, a student with a deep interest in ancient wisdom and Zen philosophy. Maya will introduce Amelia to meditation, mindfulness, and esoteric practices that will deepen her self-understanding and open her mind to new possibilities.

## Elena (Very High OK)
If Amelia has exceptionally high Occult Knowledge (OK) points, she may encounter Elena, a mysterious figure who seems to appear when Amelia least expects it. Elena will hint at hidden truths and ancient secrets, drawing Amelia deeper into the world of the occult.

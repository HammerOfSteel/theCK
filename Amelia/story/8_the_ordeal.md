## Personal Loss: Sarah's Suicide
- If Amelia's scores in most categories (AA, SI, MH, MC) are not high enough, she will face the tragedy of Sarah's suicide. This event will occur regardless of Amelia's specific choices in this chapter, as it represents the culmination of Sarah's long-term struggles and Amelia's cumulative impact on her well-being.

Setup
-Amelia receives an urgent message from Sarah, asking to meet her in a secluded spot on campus.
When Amelia arrives, she finds Sarah in a state of deep distress, tears streaming down her face.

Dialogue
- Sarah: "Amelia... I'm sorry... I didn't know who else to turn to..."
- Amelia: "Sarah, what's wrong? You're scaring me. Talk to me, please."
- Sarah: "I can't... I can't do this anymore. The pain, the emptiness... it's too much. I've tried so hard, but I just can't see a way out."
- Amelia: "Sarah, listen to me. You're not alone in this. I'm here for you, and we can get through this together."
- Sarah: "No, Amelia. You don't understand. I've been fighting this darkness for so long, and I'm just... I'm so tired. I can't keep pretending that everything's okay when it's not."
- Amelia: "I know it's hard, Sarah. Believe me, I know. But ending your life is not the answer. You have so much to live for, even if you can't see it right now."
- Sarah: "Do I? All I see is pain, all I feel is hopelessness. I've tried therapy, medication, everything... and nothing helps. I'm just a burden to everyone, including you."
- Amelia: "Sarah, you could never be a burden. Your life has value and meaning, and there are so many people who care about you, who would be devastated if they lost you."
- Sarah: "I'm sorry, Amelia. I know you're trying to help, but... I've made up my mind. I just wanted to say goodbye, and to thank you for being my friend."
- Amelia: "Sarah, no! Please, don't do this. Stay with me, we can call for help, we can figure this out..."
Outcome

Despite Amelia's desperate pleas, Sarah pulls away and runs off, leaving Amelia in a state of shock and terror.
What happens next depends on Amelia's cumulative scores:

- If Amelia's scores are too low (below a certain threshold in most categories), she is unable to reach Sarah in time, and Sarah tragically takes her own life. Amelia is left devastated, haunted by guilt and grief, questioning whether she could have done more to save her friend.

- If Amelia's scores are high enough (above a certain threshold in most categories), she is able to mobilize quickly, calling for emergency services and rallying her friends to search for Sarah. They find her just in time, and with the help of professionals, Sarah is brought to safety and given the intensive support she needs. Amelia is shaken but relieved, and this near-tragedy cements her commitment to being there for her friends and advocating for mental health.

## Impact on Amelia and the Player
Regardless of the outcome, this event is a profound and traumatic experience for Amelia, one that will shape the rest of her journey. It forces her (and the player) to confront the reality of mental illness, the limits of individual support, and the importance of community and professional resources.

- If Sarah dies, Amelia must grapple with intense grief, guilt, and a crisis of faith in herself and her path. This tragedy may cause her to re-evaluate her priorities, her relationships, and her understanding of what it means to truly help others.

- If Sarah survives, Amelia is affirmed in the importance of her friendships and her growing leadership skills. However, she is also acutely aware of how close she came to losing someone she cares about, and this brush with tragedy will likely deepen her commitment to her own mental health and that of those around her.

For the player, this event is a stark and emotional reminder of the stakes of Amelia's journey, and how their choices and actions (or lack thereof) can have profound, sometimes life-altering consequences. It's a moment that demands reflection, empathy, and a reckoning with one's own values and priorities.

In the aftermath of this ordeal, Amelia (and the player) must find a way to process this trauma, to honor Sarah's struggle, and to carry forward the lessons learned from this painful experience. The path ahead will be shaped by this pivotal moment, as Amelia continues to navigate the complexities of mental health, friendship, and personal growth in an imperfect world.

# The CK - Amelia

## Overview
"The CK - Amelia" is a visual novel that follows the protagonist, Amelia, through her transformative first year at the University of Plymouth. As she navigates academic challenges and personal growth, the story explores themes of mental health, coping strategies, and personal discovery.

## Main Characters

- **Amelia**: The protagonist, a hopeful psychology student, eager to learn but unaware of the social and personal challenges she'll encounter.
- **Ella**: Amelia's childhood best friend. A steadfast supporter and the link to Amelia's past.
- **Prof. Hawthorne**: The head of the psychology department. Stern yet fair, he becomes Amelia's mentor.
- **Lucas**: Amelia's roommate. Introverted, he introduces Amelia to Jungian psychology.
- **Zara**: A fellow student from another country, she helps Amelia understand cultural differences and racism.
- **Maya**: Interested in ancient wisdom and Zen philosophy, she helps Amelia explore spiritual and philosophical dimensions.
- **Raj**: Specializes in family systems, helping Amelia understand the complexities of familial relationships.
- **Sarah**: Struggling with severe depression, her interactions with Amelia highlight themes of mental health and the impact of personal choices on others' lives.

## Background Characters

- **Mr. & Mrs. James**: Amelia's parents, supportive yet concerned about her journey.
- **Lily**: Amelia's younger cousin, who looks up to her and seeks her guidance.
- **Tasha**: Starts as a bully but undergoes significant development throughout the story.
- **Dr. Simmons**: Teaches positive psychology, encouraging a bright outlook on life.
- **Elena**: A mature student who provides motherly wisdom and life advice.
- **Michael**: A passionate student activist, introducing Amelia to broader social issues.
- **Sophia**: Amelia's academic rival, pushing her to excel and presenting unexpected challenges.

## Chapter Summaries

### Chapter 1: Ordinary World
Amelia experiences a symbolic dream set in a London park, revisiting key childhood memories that set foundational stats for her character based on player choices.

### Chapter 2: Call to Adventure
Amelia receives her university acceptance letter, prompting preparations for her new journey in Plymouth, marked by excitement and anxiety.

### Chapter 3: Refusal
Initial challenges of settling into university life, dealing with loneliness, and adjusting to new surroundings.

### Chapter 4: Meeting with the Mentor
Amelia chooses her classes and mentors, determining her academic path and establishing key relationships in her field of study.

### Chapter 5: Crossing the Threshold - Meeting Sarah
Amelia encounters Sarah in a vulnerable state, setting the stage for a deep and impactful friendship centered around supporting Sarah through her struggles.

### Chapter 6: Tests, Allies, Enemies
Amelia deepens her university relationships through various social and academic events, facing personal and interpersonal tests.

### Chapter 7: Approach
Focus on Amelia's study group dynamics, facing academic pressures and social challenges, highlighting her leadership and conflict-resolution skills.

### Chapter 8: Ordeal - Saving Sarah
Amelia deals with critical moments in Sarah's mental health crisis, making pivotal decisions that affect both their lives significantly.

### Chapter 9: Reward
After the emotional challenges of helping Sarah, Amelia seeks personal recovery and reflection, engaging in activities that promote her well-being.

### Chapter 10: The Road Back
Amelia prepares for her final exams and projects, planning for her summer and reflecting on her academic and personal growth during her first year.

### Chapter 11: Resurrection
Amelia concludes her first year, focusing on final assessments and planning for the future, reflecting on her transformations and preparing for her return home.

### Chapter 12: Return with the Elixir
Amelia returns to London, reuniting with family and friends who notice her growth. Depending on her choices, various endings unfold, reflecting her academic, social, and personal developments throughout the year.

## Endings
Based on accumulated points and decisions, Amelia may achieve outcomes ranging from academic success to becoming a mental health advocate, or experiencing profound spiritual enlightenment. Each ending has a version that reflects whether Sarah survived or not, showcasing the deep impact of Amelia's journey and choices on her life and others'.

This document outlines the core narrative and structure of "The CK - Amelia," providing a detailed guide for further development and adaptation.
